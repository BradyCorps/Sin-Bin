Character Direction

Personality and Gameplay Summary

Short-form canon for writing, card design and mechanical exploration.

CORE FANTASY A tiny, technically gifted instigator who controls space and other people’s emotions. She wins through edge control, deception, timing and nerve—not strength.

Personality

Violetta is immaculate, observant and irritatingly self-possessed. She enjoys causing an opponent to react badly, then treating the consequence as proof that she was in control. Her confidence is quiet rather than theatrical. Genuine delight appears only when a result breaks through that control.

Gameplay Identity

Primary role: finesse instigator / tempo manipulator. Her mechanical space should reward evasive movement, drawing retaliation, exploiting opponent discipline and converting positional advantages. She should create pressure indirectly rather than win raw physical contests.

Strengths

Agility, edge control, puck evasion, provocation, timing, reading openings.

Weaknesses

Low physical power, limited reach, vulnerable when pinned or denied space.

Player feeling

Outthink the opponent, escape contact, make them overcommit, punish the reaction.

Mechanical boundary

Exact stats, costs and named abilities are not locked by this visual bible.
